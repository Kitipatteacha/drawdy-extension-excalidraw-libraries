# Excalidraw Libraries for Drawdy

Use any of the community libraries from
[libraries.excalidraw.com](https://libraries.excalidraw.com) on your Drawdy
board. Find a library, click an item, and it lands in the middle of your
view as shapes you can edit.

## What you get

- All of the community libraries, with their previews and download counts,
  searchable by name, description or author.
- Every item drawn the way Excalidraw draws it, so you see what you get
  before you add it.
- Items added as ordinary Drawdy shapes, texts, lines, arrows and drawings,
  grouped so they move as one. Change their colours, text or size like
  anything else on the board.
- Colours that follow the board: Excalidraw's palette and its blacks switch
  with the light and dark theme, and brand colours in icons stay as drawn.
- **Open file** for a library you have as a `.excalidrawlib` file, or a
  whole `.excalidraw` drawing as one item. You can also drop the file on the
  panel.

## Install

Excalidraw Libraries ships as a single file,
`drawdy-excalidraw-libraries.drawdyx`. Install it in Drawdy and the
Excalidraw Libraries icon appears at the top right of the board.

The first time it needs to, Drawdy asks you to grant two permissions: `dom`
for the button and panel, and `scene` for adding items to the board.

## How to use

### 1. Find a library

Click the Excalidraw Libraries icon. Search, or sort by **Popular** (most
downloaded first), **Newest** or **A–Z**, then click a library to open it.

### 2. Add items

Click an item and it goes onto the board in the middle of your view,
selected, so you can drag it straight to where it belongs. Or drag the item
out of the panel and drop it where you want it. If it's bigger than the
screen, the board zooms out to show it all.

### 3. Shapes or pictures

Items go in as shapes. A few use something Drawdy shapes can't show yet:
hatched fills inside freeform outlines, filled drawings, turned text, or
arrowheads like dots and bars. These are marked with a picture icon and go
in as a picture instead, so they still look right.

## Cheat sheet

| You do                              | What happens                          |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Click the Excalidraw Libraries icon | Opens the panel                       |
| Click a library                     | Shows its items                       |
| Click an item                       | Adds it to the middle of the board    |
| Drag an item onto the board         | Adds it where you drop it             |
| Open file, or drop a file           | Opens a library from your device      |
| `Esc`                               | Back to all libraries                 |

## Good to know

- Texts use Drawdy's font rather than Excalidraw's hand-drawn one, lined up
  where their author put them. The previews in the panel show them in
  Drawdy's font too.
- Lines and arrows come out smooth, as Drawdy draws them, rather than
  sketchy.
- A picture is drawn for the theme you're on when you add it, and doesn't
  switch theme with the board afterwards.
- An item with lines and shapes layered over each other can take a few
  presses of undo to remove.
- The catalogue, the library files and Drawdy's handwriting font load from
  libraries.excalidraw.com and cdn.jsdelivr.net. Nothing from your board is
  sent anywhere.
